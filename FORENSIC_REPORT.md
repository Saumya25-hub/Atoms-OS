## 1. Forensic Audit Findings

### Finding A: Un-gated VRAM Presentation (Lack of VSYNC Scanline Synchronization)
- **Location**: [rook_render.c](file:///d:/Signatures_OS/kernel/shell/rook/src/rook_render.c#L68-L87)
- **Root Cause**: `rook_render_flush()` blits dirty rectangles to physical VRAM immediately without waiting for the monitor's Vertical Retrace / VBLANK interval (`inb(0x3DA) & 0x08` / VSYNC gating).
- **Evidence**: On 60Hz/144Hz monitors, mid-frame writes cause scanline tearing and visual phase jitter.

### Finding B: Un-checked Raw MSR Access (MTRR Write-Combining Crash in VMs)
- **Location**: [vram_accel.c](file:///d:/Signatures_OS/kernel/drivers/display/vram_accel.c#L14-L32)
- **Root Cause**: `wrmsr` executed raw MSR writes without checking CPUID MTRR capability (CPUID `EAX=1`, `EDX` bit 12) or hypervisor detection (CPUID `EAX=1`, `ECX` bit 31).
- **Evidence**: VMware Workstation triggered a General Protection Fault (`#GP`) causing CPU Triple Fault shutdown.

### Finding C: RAM Backbuffers & Hardware Timers Status
- **Status**: RAM Backbuffers (`s_static_canvas`, `g_rook_backbuffer`) and Hardware Timers (`timer_get_ticks()`) are active and verified.

## 3. Risk Analysis
- High Risk: Raw MSR writes on unsupported hypervisors can trigger `#GP` faults.
- Solution: Perform strict CPUID feature check and hypervisor detection before executing MTRR MSR writes.
