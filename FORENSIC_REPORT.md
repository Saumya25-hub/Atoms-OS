## 1. Root Cause Findings

### Finding A: Canvas Wallpaper Discrepancy Between Boot & Login
- **Location**: [wallpaper_service.c](file:///d:/Signatures_OS/kernel/services/wallpaper/wallpaper_service.c#L54-L60) & [page_login.c](file:///d:/Signatures_OS/kernel/shell/rook/pages/page_login.c#L796)
- **Mechanism**: Stage 1 Boot Splash uses a pure `#000000` pitch black background. Stage 2 Login Page calls `wallpaper_service_render`, which generates a sky-blue landscape gradient (`0x0093C5FD` to `0x00E0F2FE`).

### Finding B: Partial Invalidation Split Screen During Page Transition
- **Location**: [page_login.c](file:///d:/Signatures_OS/kernel/shell/rook/pages/page_login.c#L841-L845) & [rook_core.c](file:///d:/Signatures_OS/kernel/shell/rook/src/rook_core.c#L38-L55)
- **Mechanism**: `rook_goto(ROOK_PAGE_LOGIN)` switched pages without invalidating the full screen. `page_login.c` only invalidated a $360 \times 360$ partial rect when `s_signin_alpha == 255`.
- **Result**: Physical VRAM showed a split screen: the top portion rendered the sky-blue wallpaper, while the bottom portion remained pitch black from the boot splash!

## 2. Permanent Windows NT / Linux Resolution Plan

### 1. Unified Dark Premium (#0B0F19 / #000000) Background
- Update `wallpaper_service.c` to build a unified dark premium `#0B0F19` / `#000000` background canvas matching the boot splash.

### 2. Mandatory Full Frame Invalidation on Page Transition
- Force `rook_invalidate_full()` in `rook_goto(page_id)` and `page_login_on_enter` to guarantee 100% of the display is synchronously repainted in a single frame.
